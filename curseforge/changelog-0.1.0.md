First release.

- A TV frame you can place and resize anywhere on the screen; the ForeverTV app for Windows plays the YouTube video exactly inside it, with clicks passing through to the game.
- Optional hand-painted goblin TV casing (`/ftv tv`): TUNE dial plays and pauses, the power switch hides the TV, the brass crank resizes it. Remembered between sessions.
- Play and pause from the game (`/ftv pause`, `/ftv play`), also in combat.
- Send videos with one click from YouTube with the ForeverTV - Play in WoW browser extension, or paste a link in the app's tray menu.
